# Final Direction

## Selected Direction

**Antojo Inmediato — marisquería popular contemporánea.**

> Una marisquería costera enérgica y auténtica, con fotografía irresistible, lenguaje gráfico artesanal y un flujo de pedido por WhatsApp rápido, evidente y confiable.

## Approved Principles

- Deep navy establishes trust and anchors the brand.
- Warm cream improves readability and gives the interface a handcrafted tone.
- Orange-red signals price, appetite and emphasis.
- WhatsApp green is reserved for adding products and completing the order.
- Condensed, bold display typography carries the commercial voice.
- Close, abundant food photography is the main visual argument.
- Marine line illustrations add personality without becoming a theme-park aesthetic.
- Product price, cart status and total must remain obvious.

## Content and Interaction Direction

- Primary headline: **“¿Se te antojó? Pídelo ahora”.**
- Primary path: discover product → add → review cart → provide minimum details → send through WhatsApp.
- Desktop may use a persistent or sticky order summary where space permits.
- Mobile must use progressive disclosure and a clear cart entry point; it must not be a scaled desktop page.

## Non-Binding Mockup Content

Generated product data, totals, locations, schedules, labels and decorative details are illustrative. Wireframes and final UI must use one approved source of truth.
