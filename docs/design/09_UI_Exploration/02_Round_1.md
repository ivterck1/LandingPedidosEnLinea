# Round 1

> **Outcome:** Completed. Antojo Inmediato was selected as the strongest direction. The original three-finalist rule was intentionally shortened after review because one concept showed a decisive advantage.

## Objective

Round 1 explores six different visual concepts before selecting three finalists.

This round evaluates direction, not final execution. The purpose is to compare distinct identities, page rhythm, mobile behavior and conversion clarity.

## Concepts

1. Costa Editorial.
2. Mercado Costero.
3. Caribe Moderno.
4. Marisqueria Artesanal.
5. Antojo Inmediato.
6. Brutalismo Costero.

## Mockups Per Concept

Each concept receives two externally generated mockups:

- Full landing desktop.
- Full landing mobile.

## Expected Files

For each concept:

```text
prompts/01-full-landing-desktop.md
prompts/02-full-landing-mobile.md
mockups/01-full-landing-desktop.webp
mockups/02-full-landing-mobile.webp
```

Prompts were completed and real generated evidence for the selected direction is stored in `concepts/05-antojo-inmediato/mockups/`.

## Selection Criteria

- Brand alignment.
- Appetite and product prominence.
- Order clarity.
- Mobile quality.
- WhatsApp conversion.
- Differentiation.
- Technical viability.
- Scalability.
- Accessibility risk.

## Review Checklist

- Does the full page include all required sections?
- Does the product remain protagonist?
- Does the concept feel distinct from the other five?
- Is the mobile version reorganized, not merely compressed?
- Can the user understand how to order?
- Is the cart easy to find?
- Is WhatsApp visible as the final action?
- Is the layout technically plausible?
- Are there obvious text or accessibility issues?

## Decision

Round 1 ended with the approval of Antojo Inmediato for focused refinement.
