> **Outcome:** Completed as a focused refinement of Antojo Inmediato through five coordinated views. A three-concept competition was not required.

## Objective

Round 2 refines only the three finalists selected in Round 1.

The goal is to test whether each finalist can support detailed product selection, cart behavior, form completion and WhatsApp conversion.

## What Advances

The non-selected concepts remain archived as exploration evidence. Only Antojo Inmediato advanced to detailed visual refinement.

## Refinement Scope

Round 2 may generate:

- Hero detail.
- Menu detail.
- Cart and form detail.
- Component exploration.
- Responsive refinement.
- Updated HANT Score.

## Controlled Hybridization

Hybridization is allowed only when it solves a documented problem.

Examples:

- One concept has stronger appetite, another has clearer cart behavior.
- One concept has better brand presence, another has stronger mobile flow.
- One concept has stronger differentiation, another is more scalable.

The hybrid must still feel like one coherent direction.

## HANT Score Update

After Round 2, scoring should be updated with more evidence:

- Hero impact.
- Menu scanability.
- Cart clarity.
- Form confidence.
- WhatsApp continuity.
- Mobile interaction quality.

## Output

Round 2 should produce enough evidence to choose one final direction or one controlled hybrid.
