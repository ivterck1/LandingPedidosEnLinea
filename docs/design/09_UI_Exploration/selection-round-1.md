# Selection Round 1

## Decision

**Selected direction: 05 — Antojo Inmediato.**

The six proposed concepts were reviewed as distinct visual territories. Antojo Inmediato was selected because it best combines appetite, brand personality and a direct path to ordering.

## Evaluation Summary

| Concept | Main strength | Reason it was not selected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Costa Editorial | Elegance and calm | Too restrained for immediate appetite and conversion |
| Mercado Costero | Energy and abundance | Risks visual noise and a less premium perception |
| Caribe Moderno | Fresh, tropical lightness | More generic and less ownable |
| Marisquería Artesanal | Warmth and human preparation | Less commercially forceful |
| Antojo Inmediato | Product impact and order clarity | **Selected** |
| Brutalismo Costero | High differentiation | Experimental tone could reduce trust and usability |

## Evidence

- Strong food photography creates immediate appetite.
- Navy, cream and orange produce a recognizable coastal identity.
- Condensed display typography gives the brand a direct, popular-contemporary voice.
- Prices, add actions, cart total and WhatsApp CTA are prominent.
- The visual system can scale from hero to menu, cart and order form.

## Scope Decision

A traditional three-finalist Round 2 was not required. The selected concept showed a sufficiently clear advantage and was expanded into five complementary views for controlled refinement.

## Status

Approved for final-direction definition and wireframes.
