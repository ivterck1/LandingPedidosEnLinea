# 04 Marisqueria Artesanal

## 1. Concept Name

Marisqueria Artesanal.

## 2. Creative Idea

A warm and handcrafted seafood ordering experience that emphasizes preparation, honesty and human care.

## 3. Business Alignment

Builds trust and repeat ordering by making the restaurant feel real, reliable and attentive.

## 4. User Alignment

Useful for families, frequent customers and cautious users who need confidence before sending an order.

## 5. Brand Personality

Warm, handcrafted, traditional, honest and human.

## 6. Emotional Objective

Make the user feel cared for and confident that the order will be prepared well.

## 7. Visual Principles

- Trust before spectacle.
- Preparation details support credibility.
- Menu information stays practical.
- Form and cart feel reassuring.
- Warmth must not become outdated.

## 8. Photography Direction

Prepared dishes, ingredients, subtle process moments and honest table scenes. Avoid excessive rustic styling.

## 9. Typography Direction

Human, readable and steady. Typography should feel warm without using decorative nostalgia.

## 10. Color Direction

Warm natural coastal direction. Avoid heavy brown or overly rustic palettes.

## 11. Layout Direction

Desktop uses story and menu as a connected experience. Mobile uses practical ordering blocks with gentle trust cues.

## 12. Section Rhythm

Warm and reassuring: hero with care, trust strip, specialties with preparation cues, menu, story, cart and form.

## 13. Menu Treatment

Menu should highlight ingredients and preparation. Descriptions can be slightly warmer but still short.

## 14. Cart Treatment

Cart should feel like a clear order ticket: quantities, subtotals and total are easy to confirm.

## 15. Mobile Treatment

Mobile must prioritize speed while keeping the feeling of a human restaurant. Notes and delivery type should be easy to understand.

## 16. Advantages

- Strong trust.
- Good for repeat customers.
- Human and approachable.
- Supports product story.

## 17. Risks

- Can feel less modern.
- May reduce Caribbean freshness.
- Could become too text-heavy.

## 18. Technical Viability

High. Can be built with simple layouts and lightweight media.

## 19. Scalability

High. Works well for future stories, specials, delivery notes and loyalty content.

## 20. Expected User Perception

Real, warm, careful, honest and dependable.
