# 02 Full Landing Mobile - Marisqueria Artesanal

## 1. Creative Brief

Create a complete mobile landing for Marisqueria Artesanal. The experience should feel warm, human and reliable while staying quick.

## 2. Art Direction

One-column handcrafted seafood ordering flow, warm content blocks, clear product cards, simple form and reassuring cart preview.

## 3. UX Requirements

Large touch controls, visible cart, direct add buttons, short form and WhatsApp CTA. The user should never feel lost.

## 4. Required Sections

Header, Hero, Trust strip, Specialties, Categories, Menu preview, Brand story, Cart preview, Order form preview, WhatsApp CTA, Footer.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use "Hecho al momento", "Agregar", "Tu pedido", "Total $290 MXN", "Entrega a domicilio", "Recoger", "Enviar por WhatsApp".

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Front-facing mobile landing, 9:16, no phone frame, no hand, no perspective, no horizontal overflow.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No phone mockup, no rustic clutter, no unreadable labels, no cropped sections, no fake payment checkout.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Ask for a warm mobile-first seafood ordering page without device frame and with readable Spanish labels.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 9:16`; specify flat mobile landing UI, artisanal seafood, no phone, no hand.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Request one-column mobile flow, warm trust cues and clear WhatsApp conversion.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/02-full-landing-mobile.webp`
