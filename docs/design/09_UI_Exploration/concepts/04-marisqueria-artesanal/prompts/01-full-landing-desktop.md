# 01 Full Landing Desktop - Marisqueria Artesanal

## 1. Creative Brief

Create a full desktop landing mockup for El Cangrejo Cevichero using the Marisqueria Artesanal concept. The goal is to build trust through preparation, honesty and a warm ordering experience.

## 2. Art Direction

Warm handcrafted seafood atmosphere, honest product photography, subtle preparation cues, practical hierarchy, human tone and clear order summary.

## 3. UX Requirements

The page must help users understand what is prepared, choose products, trust the total and send a WhatsApp order.

## 4. Required Sections

Header, Hero, Trust strip, Specialties, Categories, Menu preview, Brand story, Cart preview, Order form preview, WhatsApp CTA, Footer.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use realistic Spanish content: "Mariscos preparados al momento", "Recetas de la casa", Ceviche de pescado $145 MXN, Tostada artesanal $95 MXN, Coctel Campechano $165 MXN.

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Front-facing complete vertical landing, long portrait ratio around 9:16 or taller, no browser chrome, no device, no perspective, no presentation board.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No rustic overkill, no old-fashioned menu board, no browser chrome, no device frame, no lorem ipsum, no missing sections, no unreadable prices.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Request a full landing website mockup with warm handcrafted seafood direction, readable Spanish text and no device frame.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 9:16`; specify flat front-facing full landing page, artisanal seafood restaurant, no browser, no device.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Request warm section rhythm, preparation cues, clear cart preview and WhatsApp CTA.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/01-full-landing-desktop.webp`
