# 04 Menu Section - Marisqueria Artesanal

## 1. Creative Brief

Explore a menu section that communicates preparation and honest product information.

## 2. Art Direction

Warm cards, ingredient-focused photography, clear descriptions, calm category grouping and practical prices.

## 3. UX Requirements

Each card needs image, product name, short preparation-focused description, price and add button.

## 4. Required Sections

Categories and Menu preview.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use Ceviche de la casa $155 MXN, Tostada de pulpo $125 MXN, Coctel Campechano $165 MXN, Limonada $50 MXN.

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Front-facing section mockup, no device, no perspective, no cropped cards.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No cluttered menu, no old paper menu scan, no unreadable prices, no missing buttons, no lorem ipsum.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Request an artisanal seafood menu section with readable cards and honest product descriptions.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 4:5`; specify flat menu UI section, warm handcrafted seafood.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Request ingredient-focused product cards, categories and add buttons.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/04-menu-section.webp`
