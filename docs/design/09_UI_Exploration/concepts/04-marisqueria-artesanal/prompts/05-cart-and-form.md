# 05 Cart And Form - Marisqueria Artesanal

## 1. Creative Brief

Explore the cart and form for Marisqueria Artesanal with a trustworthy order-ticket feeling.

## 2. Art Direction

Warm order summary, clear quantity rows, practical form fields, reassuring messages and visible WhatsApp CTA.

## 3. UX Requirements

Show product rows, stepper controls, subtotals, total, customer name, delivery type, address and notes.

## 4. Required Sections

Cart preview, Order form preview, WhatsApp CTA.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use "Tu pedido", "1x Ceviche de la casa", "Total $280 MXN", "Notas del pedido", "Enviar por WhatsApp".

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Flat UI section, no payment gateway, no browser chrome, no device.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No credit card fields, no hidden total, no unreadable form labels, no lorem ipsum, no modal-only UI.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Request a warm cart and form mockup for WhatsApp ordering with readable Spanish fields.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 4:5`; specify artisanal order summary and simple form UI.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Request clear cart totals, customer fields and WhatsApp CTA.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/05-cart-and-form.webp`
