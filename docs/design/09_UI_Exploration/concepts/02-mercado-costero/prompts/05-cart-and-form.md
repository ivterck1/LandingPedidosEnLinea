# 05 Cart And Form - Mercado Costero

## 1. Creative Brief

Explore cart and form behavior for Mercado Costero, balancing energy with order clarity.

## 2. Art Direction

Lively order ticket feeling, strong totals, friendly form labels and prominent WhatsApp close.

## 3. UX Requirements

Show editable cart rows, total, customer name, delivery options, address when needed, notes and WhatsApp CTA.

## 4. Required Sections

Cart preview, Order form preview, WhatsApp CTA.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use "Tu pedido", "2x Ceviche Mixto", "Total $350 MXN", "Nombre", "Direccion", "Enviar por WhatsApp".

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Flat UI section, no payment gateway, no browser chrome, no device, clear order form.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No credit card checkout, no fake payment system, no hidden total, no lorem ipsum, no visual chaos.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Request a friendly cart and form section with clear WhatsApp ordering, readable Spanish text and no device frame.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 4:5`; specify colorful but clear order summary and form UI.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Request organized cart rows, visible total and strong WhatsApp CTA.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/05-cart-and-form.webp`
