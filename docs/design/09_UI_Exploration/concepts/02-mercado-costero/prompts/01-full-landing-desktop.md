# 01 Full Landing Desktop - Mercado Costero

## 1. Creative Brief

Create a full desktop landing mockup for El Cangrejo Cevichero using the Mercado Costero concept. The goal is to turn local seafood energy into a clear WhatsApp ordering experience.

## 2. Art Direction

Vibrant Mexican coastal market atmosphere, organized abundance, lively section blocks, human warmth, fresh seafood photography, expressive but readable hierarchy and strong ordering cues.

## 3. UX Requirements

The journey must feel direct: see fresh product, trust the restaurant, scan categories, add seafood, review cart, fill a short form and send WhatsApp.

## 4. Required Sections

Header, Hero, Trust strip, Specialties, Categories, Menu preview, Brand story, Cart preview, Order form preview, WhatsApp CTA, Footer.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use realistic Spanish content: "Sabor costero para compartir", "Ceviches", "Tostadas", "Cocteles", Ceviche de camaron $150 MXN, Tostada mixta $110 MXN, Agua de jamaica $45 MXN.

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Front-facing complete vertical desktop landing, long portrait ratio around 9:16 or taller, no cropped sections, no browser chrome, no device, no perspective, no presentation board.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No code, no browser UI, no laptop, no device frame, no messy market clutter, no childish fiesta graphics, no lorem ipsum, no unreadable prices, no missing sections.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Request a flat full landing mockup with vibrant but organized Mexican coastal restaurant energy and readable Spanish UI text.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 9:16`; include full landing page, front-facing, colorful Mexican seafood ordering site, no browser, no device, no perspective.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Use structured sections and emphasize organized abundance, readable menu cards and strong WhatsApp conversion.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/01-full-landing-desktop.webp`
