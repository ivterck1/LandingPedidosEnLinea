# 02 Mercado Costero

## 1. Concept Name

Mercado Costero.

## 2. Creative Idea

A vibrant Mexican coastal market translated into a clear digital ordering page: energetic, close, abundant and appetizing without becoming chaotic.

## 3. Business Alignment

Builds local character and memorability. It can increase appetite and make the brand feel approachable, which supports direct WhatsApp orders.

## 4. User Alignment

Useful for families, frequent customers and home-order users who respond to clarity, warmth and a sense of generous seafood options.

## 5. Brand Personality

Vibrant, Mexican, authentic, colorful, energetic and close.

## 6. Emotional Objective

Make the user feel that the food is fresh, lively and easy to order.

## 7. Visual Principles

- Energy must be organized.
- Product and price remain clear.
- Local personality appears through rhythm and content, not clutter.
- CTA hierarchy must stay strong.
- The page should feel human and direct.

## 8. Photography Direction

Bright seafood plates, fresh ingredients, market-like abundance, tostadas, ceviches, cocteles and colorful garnishes. Avoid messy table scenes.

## 9. Typography Direction

Readable and expressive. Titles may feel bold and local, but labels, prices and CTAs must be simple.

## 10. Color Direction

Colorful coastal-Mexican direction with controlled contrast. Avoid festival-style overload.

## 11. Layout Direction

Desktop uses energetic blocks and visual alternation. Mobile uses compact sections, strong categories and direct product scanning.

## 12. Section Rhythm

Lively and varied: energetic hero, quick trust strip, abundant specialties, strong categories, fast menu preview and practical cart/form close.

## 13. Menu Treatment

Menu should feel generous but organized. Cards can feel more tactile and lively, with visible prices and strong add actions.

## 14. Cart Treatment

Cart should act as a calm counterbalance to the energy of the menu, giving clear totals and controls.

## 15. Mobile Treatment

Mobile must avoid visual overload. Categories, add buttons and WhatsApp CTA need priority over decorative details.

## 16. Advantages

- Strong differentiation.
- High appetite potential.
- Feels local and human.
- Good fit for frequent customers.

## 17. Risks

- Can become noisy.
- Can lose premium perception.
- Needs strict accessibility control.

## 18. Technical Viability

Medium to high. Feasible, but requires careful CSS discipline and image optimization.

## 19. Scalability

Medium. It can scale if accents are systematized and product hierarchy remains consistent.

## 20. Expected User Perception

Fresh, authentic, close, generous and easy to order from.
