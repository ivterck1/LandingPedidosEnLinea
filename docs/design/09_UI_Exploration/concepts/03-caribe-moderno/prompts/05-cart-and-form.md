# 05 Cart And Form - Caribe Moderno

## 1. Creative Brief

Explore cart and form for Caribe Moderno with emphasis on ease and fresh clarity.

## 2. Art Direction

Light order summary, clean form fields, friendly hierarchy and natural WhatsApp close.

## 3. UX Requirements

Show cart products, quantity controls, subtotals, total, name, delivery type, address, notes and WhatsApp CTA.

## 4. Required Sections

Cart preview, Order form preview, WhatsApp CTA.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use "Tu pedido", "Total $315 MXN", "Nombre", "Tipo de entrega", "Notas", "Enviar por WhatsApp".

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Flat UI section, no device frame, no browser, no payment fields.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No credit card checkout, no hidden total, no unreadable labels, no lorem ipsum, no modal-only design.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Request a clean cart and form mockup for WhatsApp seafood ordering with readable Spanish labels.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 4:5`; specify flat cart and form UI, tropical modern, no device.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Request lightweight order summary, clear form and WhatsApp CTA.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/05-cart-and-form.webp`
