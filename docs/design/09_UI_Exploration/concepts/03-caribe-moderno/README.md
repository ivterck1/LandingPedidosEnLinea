# 03 Caribe Moderno

## 1. Concept Name

Caribe Moderno.

## 2. Creative Idea

A fresh, tropical and contemporary seafood ordering experience inspired by the Mexican Caribbean, designed to feel light, fast and clear.

## 3. Business Alignment

Directly supports the restaurant concept and helps position the brand as modern, fresh and casual-premium.

## 4. User Alignment

Useful for mobile users who want to browse quickly, feel appetite and send an order without friction.

## 5. Brand Personality

Fresh, tropical, clean, contemporary and light.

## 6. Emotional Objective

Make the user feel freshness, ease and immediate desire.

## 7. Visual Principles

- Lightness before decoration.
- Food first, atmosphere second.
- Clear product categories.
- Smooth transition from menu to cart.
- Friendly but mature conversion cues.

## 8. Photography Direction

Bright ceviches, citrus, cold drinks, seafood textures and clean daylight. Avoid literal beach scenes unless they support food context.

## 9. Typography Direction

Clean digital hierarchy. Titles should feel contemporary; product information should be simple and highly readable.

## 10. Color Direction

Fresh tropical coastal direction with controlled accents. It should feel bright without becoming childish.

## 11. Layout Direction

Desktop uses clean modular sections with strong visual continuity. Mobile uses one-column flow, visible product cards and accessible order actions.

## 12. Section Rhythm

Fresh and fluid: direct hero, light trust strip, playful-but-clear categories, strong menu preview and natural WhatsApp close.

## 13. Menu Treatment

Menu should be easy to scan, with balanced cards, category grouping and visible add buttons.

## 14. Cart Treatment

Cart should feel simple, light and always understandable, with total and WhatsApp flow emphasized.

## 15. Mobile Treatment

Mobile is the strongest expression of this concept. It should feel like the natural primary experience, not a reduced desktop.

## 16. Advantages

- Strong brand alignment.
- Good mobile potential.
- Balanced appetite and clarity.
- Broad audience appeal.

## 17. Risks

- Could become generic if tropical cues are too safe.
- Needs distinctive details to feel ownable.

## 18. Technical Viability

High. Fits the current tech stack and can remain lightweight.

## 19. Scalability

High. Can support more categories, delivery logic, promotions and future PWA improvements.

## 20. Expected User Perception

Fresh, modern, easy, tropical and trustworthy.
