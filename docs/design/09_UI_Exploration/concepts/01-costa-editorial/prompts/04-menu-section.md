# 04 Menu Section - Costa Editorial

## 1. Creative Brief

Explore the menu section for Costa Editorial, focusing on scanability, premium product cards and clear prices.

## 2. Art Direction

Curated menu grid, elegant cards, calm spacing, refined seafood photography and restrained category controls.

## 3. UX Requirements

Products must show image, name, description, price in MXN and "Agregar" button. Categories must be easy to scan.

## 4. Required Sections

Categories and Menu preview.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use Ceviches, Tostadas, Cocteles, Especialidades, Bebidas. Include products such as Ceviche Verde $155 MXN and Tostada de atun $95 MXN.

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Front-facing UI section, no device, no perspective, no cropped cards.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No lorem ipsum, no unreadable prices, no random food, no missing add buttons, no cluttered grid.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Ask for a focused menu section mockup with readable labels and realistic seafood product cards.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 16:10` or `--ar 4:5`; specify flat menu UI section and readable prices.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Request a clean product card section with categories, prices and add buttons.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/04-menu-section.webp`
