# 01 Full Landing Desktop - Costa Editorial

## 1. Creative Brief

Create a full desktop landing mockup for El Cangrejo Cevichero using the Costa Editorial concept. The business objective is to increase direct WhatsApp orders. The user objective is to understand the restaurant, browse seafood, review a cart preview and feel ready to order. The audience includes couples, tourists and customers looking for a premium but simple seafood experience.

## 2. Art Direction

Elegant editorial composition, calm spacing, premium seafood photography, restrained coastal atmosphere, large product moments, clear hierarchy and quiet confidence. Use refined Spanish headings, spacious sections and a polished restaurant editorial rhythm.

## 3. UX Requirements

The page must guide users from appetite to order: hero, trust, specialties, categories, menu preview, story, cart, form and WhatsApp CTA. Cart preview and total must feel precise and easy to find.

## 4. Required Sections

Header, Hero, Trust strip, Specialties, Categories, Menu preview, Brand story, Cart preview, Order form preview, WhatsApp CTA, Footer.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use realistic Spanish content: "Mariscos frescos del Caribe mexicano", "Ver menu", "Pedir por WhatsApp", Ceviche Caribe $165 MXN, Tostada de atun $95 MXN, Coctel de camaron $145 MXN. No lorem ipsum.

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Front-facing complete vertical landing page, long portrait ratio around 9:16 or taller, no cropped sections, no browser chrome, no device, no perspective, no presentation board.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No code, no browser chrome, no laptop, no phone frame, no perspective, no collage, no lorem ipsum, no unreadable fake text, no childish seafood mascot, no missing sections, no generic template.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Request a front-facing full-page website mockup with all sections visible in one long vertical image. Ask for short readable Spanish text and no device frame.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 9:16`. Include: flat front-facing full landing page, editorial seafood restaurant, no browser UI, no laptop, no phone, no perspective.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Use structured prompt blocks. Specify complete vertical website mockup, editorial premium seafood direction, readable Spanish labels and no device frame.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/01-full-landing-desktop.webp`
