# 05 Cart And Form - Costa Editorial

## 1. Creative Brief

Explore the cart and order form for Costa Editorial. The goal is trust, precision and WhatsApp continuity.

## 2. Art Direction

Quiet premium order summary, clear totals, refined inputs and restrained confirmation area.

## 3. UX Requirements

Show cart items, quantities, subtotals, total, name field, delivery type, conditional address, notes and WhatsApp CTA.

## 4. Required Sections

Cart preview, Order form preview, WhatsApp CTA.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use Spanish labels: "Tu pedido", "Subtotal", "Total", "Nombre", "Entrega a domicilio", "Recoger", "Enviar por WhatsApp".

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Front-facing UI section, no browser chrome, no device frame, technically plausible form layout.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No fake checkout gateway, no payment form, no credit card fields, no lorem ipsum, no hidden total.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Request a cart and form UI mockup, flat front-facing, with readable Spanish labels and visible total.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 4:5`; specify order summary and WhatsApp form section, no browser, no device.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Request clear ecommerce-like cart and lightweight customer form for seafood WhatsApp ordering.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/05-cart-and-form.webp`
