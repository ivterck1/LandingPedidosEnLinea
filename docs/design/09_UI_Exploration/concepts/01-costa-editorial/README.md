# 01 Costa Editorial

## 1. Concept Name

Costa Editorial.

## 2. Creative Idea

An elegant, calm and spacious seafood landing that feels like a premium editorial feature translated into an ordering experience.

## 3. Business Alignment

Supports higher perceived quality and trust, helping the restaurant justify a casual-premium position while keeping WhatsApp as the conversion goal.

## 4. User Alignment

Works for users who need clarity, confidence and a pleasant pace before ordering. It is especially useful for couples, tourists and customers comparing options.

## 5. Brand Personality

Elegant, editorial, calm, premium, spacious and product-focused.

## 6. Emotional Objective

Make the user feel appetite through confidence, freshness and restraint.

## 7. Visual Principles

- Product photography leads every major decision.
- Sections have generous breathing room.
- Copy is short and curated.
- CTAs are clear but not visually noisy.
- The page should feel refined without slowing the order.

## 8. Photography Direction

Use natural light, close seafood details, clean table surfaces, citrus, tostadas and plated ceviches. Images should feel real, quiet and carefully composed.

## 9. Typography Direction

Editorial hierarchy with refined titles and very readable interface labels. Text must remain short enough for image generation tools to render plausibly.

## 10. Color Direction

Restrained coastal tones. Color should frame the food and support hierarchy, not dominate the concept.

## 11. Layout Direction

Desktop uses wide editorial sections and calm transitions. Mobile uses a one-column sequence with strong product moments and clear CTAs.

## 12. Section Rhythm

Slow and intentional: strong hero, trust strip, curated specialties, focused menu preview, quiet brand story and clear ordering close.

## 13. Menu Treatment

Menu cards should feel like curated recommendations. Product names, prices and add buttons must be visible without crowding.

## 14. Cart Treatment

Cart preview should feel precise and reassuring, with totals and quantities treated as key trust signals.

## 15. Mobile Treatment

Mobile should preserve elegance while reducing distance between menu, cart and WhatsApp. Large tap areas and short content blocks are required.

## 16. Advantages

- Strong premium perception.
- High trust potential.
- Good accessibility potential.
- Scales well with more products.
- Strong fit for case study documentation.

## 17. Risks

- Could feel too quiet for immediate conversion.
- Depends heavily on strong photography.
- May underrepresent the more casual side of the brand.

## 18. Technical Viability

High. Feasible with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Main requirement is disciplined image optimization.

## 19. Scalability

High. The restrained system can support more categories, product details and future features.

## 20. Expected User Perception

Fresh, premium, trustworthy and carefully prepared.
