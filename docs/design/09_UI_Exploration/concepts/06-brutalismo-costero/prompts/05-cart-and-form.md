# 05 Cart And Form - Brutalismo Costero

## 1. Creative Brief

Explore a cart and form section for Brutalismo Costero that makes conversion clear inside a bold visual system.

## 2. Art Direction

High-contrast order summary, strong total block, bold form fields and unmistakable WhatsApp CTA.

## 3. UX Requirements

Show cart rows, quantity controls, subtotals, total, customer name, delivery type, address when needed, notes and WhatsApp CTA.

## 4. Required Sections

Cart preview, Order form preview, WhatsApp CTA.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use "Tu orden", "Total $340 MXN", "Nombre", "Entrega", "Notas", "Enviar por WhatsApp".

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Flat UI section, no browser chrome, no device, no payment gateway, readable contrast.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No credit card checkout, no unreadable form, no hidden total, no tiny controls, no lorem ipsum, no device frame.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Request a high-contrast cart and form UI with clear total and WhatsApp CTA.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 4:5`; specify brutalist order summary and form UI, no browser, no device.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Request readable dark cart, strong total hierarchy and clear WhatsApp action.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/05-cart-and-form.webp`
