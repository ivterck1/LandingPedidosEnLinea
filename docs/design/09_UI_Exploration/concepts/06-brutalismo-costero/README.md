# 06 Brutalismo Costero

## 1. Concept Name

Brutalismo Costero.

## 2. Creative Idea

An experimental, dark and high-contrast seafood landing that tests how bold the brand can become while still supporting ordering.

## 3. Business Alignment

Creates strong differentiation and case study value. It may help the restaurant feel memorable and premium if usability remains intact.

## 4. User Alignment

Useful for users attracted to distinctive digital experiences, but it must still help all users understand menu, cart and WhatsApp flow.

## 5. Brand Personality

Experimental, dark, bold, premium and high-contrast.

## 6. Emotional Objective

Make the user feel surprise, confidence and curiosity without losing appetite.

## 7. Visual Principles

- High impact but clear order path.
- Contrast supports hierarchy.
- Product remains recognizable and appetizing.
- Experimental layout must stay technically feasible.
- WhatsApp action must not be hidden.

## 8. Photography Direction

Dramatic seafood photography with texture, contrast, dark surfaces and precise lighting. Avoid making food look heavy or unappetizing.

## 9. Typography Direction

Bold, structured and editorial. Readability is mandatory, especially for menu and prices.

## 10. Color Direction

Dark and high contrast with controlled coastal accents. Avoid reducing freshness.

## 11. Layout Direction

Desktop can use asymmetry and bold blocks. Mobile must simplify into a clear one-column flow with strong CTA hierarchy.

## 12. Section Rhythm

Impactful and tense: bold hero, compressed proof, striking specialties, structured menu, strong cart close.

## 13. Menu Treatment

Menu cards should feel premium and graphic, but prices and add buttons must remain obvious.

## 14. Cart Treatment

Cart should cut through the experimental environment with clear totals, quantity controls and WhatsApp continuity.

## 15. Mobile Treatment

Mobile must reduce experimentation where it harms ordering. Touch targets and readability are non-negotiable.

## 16. Advantages

- Highly distinctive.
- Strong case study value.
- Memorable and premium.
- Tests the limits of the brand.

## 17. Risks

- May feel too experimental.
- Can hurt freshness if too dark.
- Accessibility risk is higher.
- Conversion may suffer if hierarchy is too unusual.

## 18. Technical Viability

Medium. Feasible, but needs careful responsive and accessibility control.

## 19. Scalability

Medium. Requires a strict system to avoid inconsistency.

## 20. Expected User Perception

Bold, premium, different and memorable, while still understandable.
