# 05 Antojo Inmediato

> **Status: Selected final direction.** Approved on 21 July 2026 and expanded into five coordinated mockup views.

## 1. Concept Name

Antojo Inmediato.

## 2. Creative Idea

A bold and fast ordering experience designed for hungry users who want to see, choose and send a WhatsApp order quickly.

## 3. Business Alignment

Maximizes conversion by reducing hesitation and making the ordering path obvious.

## 4. User Alignment

Useful for home-order users and frequent customers who prioritize speed, clear prices and direct action.

## 5. Brand Personality

Bold, fast, direct, commercial and conversion-oriented.

## 6. Emotional Objective

Make the user feel immediate appetite and confidence to act.

## 7. Visual Principles

- CTAs are highly visible.
- Product images create fast desire.
- Prices are easy to compare.
- Cart and total are prominent.
- Sections avoid unnecessary explanation.

## 8. Photography Direction

High-impact closeups of ceviches, tostadas, cocteles and specialties. Food should look ready to order.

## 9. Typography Direction

Bold and scannable. Headlines, prices and CTAs should be readable in seconds.

## 10. Color Direction

Action-oriented and appetizing. Intensity is allowed but must not feel cheap or chaotic.

## 11. Layout Direction

Desktop uses strong ordering blocks and repeated conversion cues. Mobile uses direct cards, large buttons and immediate cart access.

## 12. Section Rhythm

Fast and persuasive: strong hero, quick proof, specialties, menu preview, cart and form with minimal delay.

## 13. Menu Treatment

Menu should behave like a high-conversion product list. Image, name, price and add button must be impossible to miss.

## 14. Cart Treatment

Cart should be prominent and action-focused, making the total and WhatsApp CTA feel ready.

## 15. Mobile Treatment

Mobile should support one-handed ordering with large targets, short sections and visible progress toward WhatsApp.

## 16. Advantages

- Strong conversion potential.
- Clear mobile flow.
- Easy to understand.
- Good for users with immediate intent.

## 17. Risks

- Can feel too promotional.
- May reduce premium perception.
- Could sacrifice emotional depth for speed.

## 18. Technical Viability

High. Feasible with simple layout logic and strong hierarchy.

## 19. Scalability

Medium to high. Requires discipline so CTAs do not overwhelm future content.

## 20. Expected User Perception

Fast, appetizing, practical and easy to order from.
