# 05 Cart And Form - Antojo Inmediato

## 1. Creative Brief

Explore a cart and form section that makes the order feel ready to send.

## 2. Art Direction

Prominent total, direct order rows, large WhatsApp CTA, minimal form friction and clear error-ready areas.

## 3. UX Requirements

Show products, quantities, subtotals, total, required customer fields and final WhatsApp action.

## 4. Required Sections

Cart preview, Order form preview, WhatsApp CTA.

## 5. Content Guidance

Use "Tu carrito", "Total $330 MXN", "Completa tus datos", "Enviar por WhatsApp", "Recoger" and "Domicilio".

## 6. Technical Mockup Requirements

Flat UI section, no browser chrome, no device, no payment gateway.

## 7. Negative Prompt

No credit card checkout, no hidden CTA, no small buttons, no unreadable labels, no lorem ipsum.

## 8. GPT Image Adapter

Request a conversion-focused cart and form mockup with large WhatsApp CTA and visible total.

## 9. Midjourney Adapter

Use `--ar 4:5`; specify bold cart and form UI for WhatsApp ordering, no browser.

## 10. Flux Adapter

Request clear total hierarchy, short form and final action emphasis.

## 11. Output Filename

`mockups/05-cart-and-form.webp`
