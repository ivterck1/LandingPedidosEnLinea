# 04 — Final Direction

## Outcome

Antojo Inmediato is the approved visual direction for El Cangrejo Cevichero.

## Visual Rules

| Element | Approved direction |
| --- | --- |
| Foundation | Deep navy and warm cream |
| Commercial accent | Orange-red |
| Conversion accent | WhatsApp green, used sparingly |
| Typography | Bold condensed display face plus highly readable supporting text |
| Photography | Close, honest, abundant and appetizing seafood |
| Illustration | Hand-drawn marine motifs as secondary texture |
| Components | Strong outlines, clear prices, direct labels and visible states |

## Page Rhythm

1. Header and immediate order access.
2. Appetite-led hero.
3. Trust signals.
4. Specialties and categories.
5. Scannable menu.
6. Brand story and preparation cues.
7. Cart review.
8. Minimum-data order form.
9. WhatsApp completion.
10. Operational information and footer.

## Mobile Behavior

- Design from a real phone viewport, not by shrinking desktop.
- Keep one dominant action per view.
- Collapse navigation into an accessible menu.
- Keep cart quantity and total reachable without blocking content.
- Stack form fields and maintain large touch targets.
- Preserve food impact while limiting decorative competition.

## Wireframe Constraints

- Wireframes define behavior and hierarchy before final styling.
- All product data must come from one consistent catalog.
- Totals must be deterministic and derived from cart state.
- The WhatsApp message must reflect exactly what the user reviewed.
- Empty, loading, validation and success states must be represented.
- Branch, delivery and schedule information remains pending confirmation.

## Remaining Risks

- Excessive decorative density could harm scanning.
- Condensed display type must not be used for long body copy.
- Green and orange need semantic discipline.
- Generated imagery cannot be treated as final licensed product photography.

## Status

Approved. Ready for Phase 10 — Wireframes.
